# Off-Page SEO Checklist — Siddhi's Coaching Classes

Work through top to bottom. The Google Business Profile (GBP) and citations move
rankings for "coaching classes chembur / near me" faster than anything on the website.

## 0. Canonical NAP (use this EXACT text everywhere)

> **Siddhi's Coaching Classes**
> Shop No. 5, 6 & 7, Veena Serene Building, B-Wing, Shell Colony, Sahakar Nagar Road No. 4, Chembur, Mumbai – 400071
> +91 95943 45743
> https://siddhiscoachingclasses.in

Byte-for-byte identical across every listing. NAP inconsistency is the #1 reason
local businesses with many citations still don't rank.

## 1. Google Business Profile (already linked — now optimise it)

- [ ] Primary category: **Tutoring service**. Secondaries: Coaching center, Educational institution, Test preparation center, Learning center, Tutor
- [ ] Website field → point to **https://siddhiscoachingclasses.in** (NOT the vercel.app URL)
- [ ] Create a **second profile for Centre 2** (Adarsha Vidyalaya address) once Centre 1 is verified
- [ ] Drag the map pin onto the exact building entrance
- [ ] Services: add each as separate entries — SSC Tuition, Class 8/9/10 Tuition, HSC Science, HSC Commerce, NEET Coaching, JEE Coaching, MHT-CET Coaching, CA Foundation
- [ ] Description (750 chars): mention Chembur, Shell Colony, since 2003, SSC, HSC Science, HSC Commerce, NEET, JEE, MH-CET, CA Foundation — human-readable, no keyword stuffing
- [ ] Hours incl. lunch break + Sunday; attributes: online classes, in-person, UPI/cash accepted
- [ ] Photos: 5 exterior (with signage), 10–15 classrooms, faculty, toppers; refresh monthly
- [ ] Q&A: seed 10–15 real parent questions and answer them yourself (allowed)
- [ ] Posts 2–3×/week: admissions open, toppers, demo class invites
- [ ] **Never** add keywords to the business name ("Best SSC Coaching…") — suspension risk

## 2. Reviews (the biggest local ranking lever)

- [ ] WhatsApp the direct review link to every parent after PTMs / fee payments
- [ ] Target: 10 reviews by month 2, 50+ per centre within 12 months, then 2–3/month forever
- [ ] Ask parents to naturally mention the subject, class, teacher name and "Chembur" in the review
- [ ] Marathi/Hindi reviews count fully — encourage them
- [ ] Reply to every review within 48h (subtle keywords in replies: "…our Class 10 SSC batch…")
- [ ] Never buy reviews or collect a burst of 50 in a week — Google's filter will remove them
